# Hostel Laundry Management Web App — Final Summary

Your project is a free, QR-based hostel laundry booking and supervision system for 100+ students, designed to ensure fair duty rotation and safe management of the laundry room.

## 1. Core requirements

| Feature               | Final decision                      |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Operating days        | Saturday and Sunday                 |
| Operating hours       | 8:00 AM – 2:00 AM the following day |
| Students              | 100+                                |
| Supervision shift     | 30 minutes                          |
| Supervisors per shift | 2 students                          |
| Booking and duty      | Linked                              |
| QR verification       | Booking check-in and duty handover  |
| Budget                | ₹0/month target                     |

## 2. How it works

Student logs in

Receives an eligible duty assignment

Two students per 30-minute shift

Books a laundry slot

Must not overlap with their duty

Scans QR codes

Booking verification and duty check-in

Verified handover

Incoming and outgoing supervisors confirm responsibility

## 3. Fair duty rotation

The scheduler will:

- Prioritize students who have completed zero duties in the current rotation.
- Balance completed duties and outstanding assignments.
- Rotate early-morning and late-night shifts fairly.
- Prevent overlapping assignments.
- Record missed duties and approved swaps.
- Require verified attendance and handover before marking a duty complete.

Capacity: 72 half-hour shifts per weekend × 2 supervisors = 144 duty assignments per weekend. That is enough capacity for 100 students to complete one duty each, provided all shifts are staffed and everyone is available.

## 4. Database and technology

The database will contain:

- Students and roles
- Laundry machines
- Laundry bookings
- Duty shifts and assignments
- Booking eligibility
- QR tokens
- Shift handovers
- Incidents and duty swaps
- Audit logs
